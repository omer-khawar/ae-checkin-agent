## What these numbers mean (and do not)

Both answer keys were written by the Interim team **alongside the rules they test**: the persona outputs were derived by hand from the rule tables, and the chart-note cases came with the scanner's lexicon. 100% here is therefore a **regression test**: the deployed system, end to end over HTTP (kiosk endpoints, rules engine, persistence, drafts, de-duplication, SAE clock, scanner), reproduces the documented rules exactly. It is not an accuracy estimate and not a clinical validation; the PRO-CTCAE composite to CTCAE grade mapping is itself an approximation (`docs/WHAT_IT_GETS_WRONG.md`).

For an independent test on text the scanner was **not** tuned on, see [`docs/eval/synthea_results.md`](synthea_results.md): the same scanner and con-med matcher scored against Synthea-generated synthetic EHR notes and medication records, where recall is markedly lower and reported as such.

Conventions: a tier with no expected and no predicted cases reports 1.00 by convention; the grades compared are the engine's provisional CTCAE grades per item (CLAUDE.md §3.4(b)). All participants and notes are synthetic.

Vitals: 396 of the 396 persona vitals samples replayed are **simulated** (mock source, `isMeasured: false`, exported as VSMETHOD `Simulated`). Corroboration fires only for personas that turn on the admin "treat simulated as measured" setting; the counterfactuals replay the same inputs with it off.
