# Second-reader evaluation (Gemini)

> **Gemini reads, rules decide, people confirm. Nothing is accepted without a word-for-word quote.** Both texts are SYNTHETIC. Generated by `scripts/eval_reader.py`; do not edit by hand. `eval_reader.py --check` recomputes this file. Replay only: no API key is read and no network is used.

## What this is, and is not

The key (`packages/rules/data/reader_answer_key.json`) holds **2 cases** written by the team after the playtest. That is a **smoke test, not an accuracy estimate**: it shows the reader can add a finding the scanner cannot reach, with its quote, and that negated mentions do not become side effects. It does not say how often it will. The scanner's own accuracy is measured on the 43-case chart-scan key and on Synthea (`docs/eval/synthea_results.md`), with no model involved.
